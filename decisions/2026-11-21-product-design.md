# product design

- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Requested by:** project owner
- **Area:** process

## Feedback

We are designing a product here, a new creation: an AI product for video editors in the
capoeira domain. Add the design of the product to the codebase, together with the decisions
and everything said along the way.

## Decision

[PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) is the product design:
- who it is for (capoeira groups, their editors, the players on camera, contributors);
- the user journey (bring footage → describe → version → feedback → finish, clean, archive);
- the core concepts and where each lives in the code;
- seven product principles;
- a feature map with honest status (built / next / later);
- what is out of scope.

It sits next to STYLE.md (visual rules) and `decisions/` (history). A change to the product's
scope, users or principles updates PRODUCT.md and adds a decision.
